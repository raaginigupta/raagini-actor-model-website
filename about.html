About

Raagini Gupta is a New York City-based actor and model currently training at the Lee Strasberg Theatre & Film Institute.

Raised in the Bay Area in a South Asian family, she grew up surrounded by music, movement, and storytelling. Though creativity was always a meaningful part of her life, she initially pursued a different path, studying environmental science and later building a career in technology consulting.

While she found fulfillment in challenging herself professionally, storytelling remained the one pursuit she could never fully leave behind. In 2024, she moved to New York City without knowing anyone and enrolled in her first acting class. What began as a leap of faith quickly became a calling.

Today, Raagini is drawn to work that explores vulnerability, resilience, and the complexities of human connection. She approaches each role with curiosity, discipline, and a deep appreciation for the opportunity to tell meaningful stories.
